About

Evidence-driven leadership for public systems.

Dr. Gati works at the intersection of education policy, data systems, program delivery, and governance. His experience spans national-level coordination, monitoring and evaluation, research, teaching, safeguards, and reporting for senior leaders and development partners.

He brings a multidisciplinary background in computer applied technology, information security, education systems, child protection, gender, and data analysis, with a record of peer-reviewed research in privacy-preserving computation and cyber-physical-social systems.
